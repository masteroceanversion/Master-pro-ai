🎧 MASTERPRO AI

AI-Powered Music Mastering Studio

MASTERPRO AI is an intelligent music mastering application designed to help independent artists, producers, and musicians achieve professional-sounding audio without needing an expensive studio.

Upload your track, let the AI analyse your sound, and master your music with powerful audio processing tools.

🚀 Features

* AI Music Mastering: Automatically analyses and enhances uploaded tracks.
* Professional Sound: Improves loudness, clarity, balance, and overall sound quality.
* Smart EQ: Optimises frequency balance.
* Dynamic Compression: Helps create a controlled, consistent sound.
* Stereo Enhancement: Enhances stereo width and depth.
* Loudness Optimisation: Helps prepare tracks for streaming platforms.
* Audio Preview: Listen to your original and mastered versions.
* Download Mastered Tracks: Export your finished music.
* Mobile Friendly: Designed to work on smartphones, tablets, and computers.

🎵 How It Works

1. Open MASTERPRO AI.
2. Upload your original song.
3. Select your preferred mastering style.
4. Let the AI analyse your audio.
5. Start the mastering process.
6. Preview your mastered track.
7. Download your final audio.
